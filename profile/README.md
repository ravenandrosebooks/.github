# Raven and Rose Books

> Building more meaningful ways to discover books, understand reading taste, and connect readers with stories that stay with them.

Raven and Rose Books is an independent bookselling and literary technology organization. We bring together thoughtful bookselling, curated reading experiences, and reader-centered software to help people find books with greater confidence and understand the patterns behind what they love to read.

Our work begins with a simple belief: finding the right book should feel personal. That belief shapes both the books and collections available through Raven and Rose Books and the technology we are building through ReaderGenome.

## Our mission

We want to make book discovery more human, more transparent, and more meaningful.

That means:

- treating reading taste as a rich and evolving identity, not a list of genres;
- combining careful human curation with responsible, explainable technology;
- helping readers understand *why* a book may resonate with them;
- creating experiences that encourage curiosity, reflection, and a lasting relationship with books; and
- building a welcoming community around the many different reasons people read.

### ReaderGenome

[ReaderGenome](https://readergenome.com/) is a reader identity and book-discovery application created by Raven and Rose Books. It is designed to move beyond generic recommendation lists by learning *why* someone reads and showing the reasoning behind the books it surfaces.

ReaderGenome uses an adaptive assessment and ongoing reading behavior to build an evolving reader profile. Its identity model includes:

- **20 Reader Genes** — measurable traits such as Curiosity, Empathy, Wonder, Logic, and Imagination;
- **12 Core Genomes** — stable reader identities that describe the deeper motivation behind reading;
- **30 Archetypes** — expressions of a Core Genome shaped by a reader's dominant genes; and
- **an evolution timeline** — a record of how a reader's profile changes through new answers, choices, and reading experiences.

The application is being built to help readers:

- discover and understand their reader identity;
- receive book matches with clear, evidence-based reasoning;
- build a personal library and track reading progress;
- see how their tastes evolve over time; and
- take part in a reader community built around thoughtful conversation.

ReaderGenome is currently in active development. Visit [readergenome.com](https://readergenome.com/) for product news and release updates. As public resources become available, the site will also serve as the central home for API access, technical documentation, product policies, privacy information, and other application resources.

## How Raven and Rose Books and ReaderGenome connect

Raven and Rose Books and ReaderGenome are two parts of the same mission.

| Raven and Rose Books | ReaderGenome |
| --- | --- |
| Human curation and bookselling | Reader identity and explainable discovery |
| Themed collections and tangible reading experiences | Adaptive profiles and personalized book matches |
| A direct connection between books and readers | Technology that learns from a reader's choices over time |

Raven and Rose Books provides the bookselling perspective, curation, and reader-first values behind the organization. ReaderGenome extends those values into software, creating a bridge between the feeling of being understood by a thoughtful bookseller and the reach of a modern reading platform.

## Contributing

We welcome thoughtful participation from readers, booksellers, librarians, writers, designers, researchers, and software builders. Contribution opportunities will vary by repository and by the stage of each project.

If a repository is open for contributions:

1. Read its `README`, contribution guide, and code of conduct before beginning.
2. Search existing issues and discussions before opening a new one.
3. For a substantial change, start with an issue so the approach can be discussed before work begins.
4. Keep pull requests focused, explain the reader or community benefit, and include appropriate tests or documentation.
5. Be respectful of reader privacy, accessibility, intellectual property, and the rights attached to book data and artwork.

Bug reports, documentation improvements, accessibility feedback, and carefully scoped feature proposals are especially valuable. Please use the issue templates and contribution instructions in the relevant repository when they are available.

## Principles for our projects

Across our work, we aim to build with:

- **Readers first** — features should serve a real reader need.
- **Explainability** — conclusions and book matches should come with understandable reasons.
- **Privacy and care** — reading history and identity data deserve thoughtful protection.
- **Accessibility** — literary experiences should welcome as many people as possible.
- **Respect for creative work** — book metadata, descriptions, covers, and other assets must be used responsibly and with attention to rights.
- **Evidence over hype** — we prefer honest product language, testable claims, and transparent limitations.
- **Evolution over labels** — readers change, and the tools we build should leave room for that growth.
